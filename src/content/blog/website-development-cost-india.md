## What Factors Actually Affect Website Pricing?

Four technical variables determine whether a project stays at ₹19,999 or moves toward custom enterprise engineering:

- **Custom UI/UX vs. Pre-Built Templates:** Bespoke layouts built around your customer journey convert significantly better than generic multi-purpose themes bloated with unused scripts.
- **CMS & Dynamic Content Requirements:** Adding a blog, dynamic service catalog, portfolio manager, or multi-author publishing workflow requires structured content modeling.
- **Third-Party Integrations:** Connecting CRM pipelines, WhatsApp Business triggers, Razorpay/Stripe payments, or ERP APIs adds backend engineering and security testing scope.
- **Technical SEO & Performance Engineering:** Pre-rendered static HTML, structured JSON-LD schema, self-referencing canonicals, and compressed media ensure Google indexes every page from day one.

## Hidden Costs to Clarify Before Starting

A trustworthy agency separates core build fees from third-party recurring expenses so there are zero billing surprises later:

- **Domain Registration & Cloud Hosting:** Usually billed annually or monthly directly to your business account so you maintain 100% ownership.
- **Paid APIs & Commercial Plugins:** Email SMTP gateways, premium fonts, payment gateway transaction fees, or third-party SaaS tools.
- **Ongoing Website Maintenance:** Security patches, daily backups, uptime monitoring, and content edits (typically **₹5,999/month** for Standard Care or **₹24,999/month** for Proactive SLA with dedicated developer hours).
- **GST:** Applicable taxes under Indian invoicing regulations.
