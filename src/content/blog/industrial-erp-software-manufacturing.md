Most mid-sized manufacturing plants run on a fragile patchwork of spreadsheets, accounting packages, and WhatsApp updates between stores, production, and dispatch. As order volume grows, that manual gap creates raw material shortages, inaccurate costing, delayed deliveries, and invisible inventory leakage.

A tailored **Industrial ERP platform** connects procurement, stores, production planning, quality control, and dispatch in real time—without forcing your factory into the rigid, expensive licensing models of legacy ERP vendors.

## 5 High-Impact Modules Every Manufacturing ERP Needs

- **Multi-Level Bill of Materials (BOM):** Define exact raw material ratios, sub-assemblies, wastage allowances, and versioned revisions per SKU.
- **Material Requirements Planning (MRP):** Automatically calculate raw material shortfalls whenever a new sales order enters production, triggering timely purchase indents.
- **Real-Time Stores & Batch Inventory:** Track inward GRNs, bin locations, supplier lot numbers, reorder thresholds, and stock issued to specific work orders.
- **Shop-Floor Production & Job Work Tracking:** Monitor stage-wise progress, machine utilization, scrap logs, and outsourced job-work challans.
- **GST-Ready Dispatch & Margin Analytics:** Link actual raw material consumption and labor costs back to each finished batch so leadership sees true order profitability.

> **Why Custom Modular ERP Wins:** Off-the-shelf per-seat ERP licenses often charge steep annual fees for every floor supervisor and storekeeper—discouraging full team adoption. Custom-owned ERP platforms scale across unlimited plant users with zero recurring per-seat licensing penalties.

## A Proven Phased Rollout Strategy

Successful factory digitization happens in controlled phases so daily production never stops:

1. **Phase 1 — Procurement & Stores Control:** Digitize supplier POs, GRNs, and real-time raw material stock ledgers.
2. **Phase 2 — BOM, Work Orders & Shop Floor:** Connect production batches directly to stores issue slips and wastage tracking.
3. **Phase 3 — Quality Control, Dispatch & Executive Dashboards:** Automate dispatch challans, invoicing, and live management KPI dashboards.
