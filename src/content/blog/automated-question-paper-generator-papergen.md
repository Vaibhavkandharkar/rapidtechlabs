Ask any school teacher or coaching institute coordinator what drains their evenings before mid-terms and mock tests, and the answer is almost always the same: **typing, aligning, and reformatting question papers in Microsoft Word.** Margins break, numbering shifts, multiple paper sets require manual shuffling, and institution headers have to be re-checked on every test.

That exact operational bottleneck is why we engineered **PaperGen**—RapidTechLabs' cloud-hosted document and question paper generation platform, live at [paper-gen-five.vercel.app](https://paper-gen-five.vercel.app/).

## What Makes Manual Question Paper Creation So Slow?

- **Repetitive Layout Formatting:** Fixing MCQ columns, mark allocations, and page breaks takes longer than selecting the actual questions.
- **Creating Multiple Exam Sets (Set A / B / C):** Preventing copying in exam halls requires shuffling sections while keeping difficulty balanced.
- **Inconsistent Branding Across Faculty:** Different teachers produce papers with mismatched fonts, missing logos, and inconsistent instruction blocks.
- **Zero Central Archive:** Last year's question papers disappear on personal laptops instead of building a reusable academic question bank.

## How PaperGen Streamlines Exam & Worksheet Production

PaperGen replaces brittle word-processor formatting with structured, automated document generation:

1. **Standardized Academic Templates:** Automatically apply your school or coaching institute header, exam code, duration, subject metadata, and mark distribution rules.
2. **Structured Sections & Blueprint Control:** Organize Section A (MCQs), Section B (Short Answers), and Section C (Analytical Problems) with automatic marks totaling.
3. **Vectorized Print-Ready PDFs:** Export crisp, A4-aligned PDFs ready for immediate photocopying or digital student distribution.
4. **Instant Cloud Access:** Runs directly in modern web browsers via Vercel cloud hosting with zero local software installation.
