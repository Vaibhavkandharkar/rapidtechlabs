### Why Does Cost Vary So Widely Between Proposals?

- **Document Parsing & Chunking (30%–40% of RAG Effort):** Clean Markdown files take hours to index; scanned manufacturing specs, multi-column financial tables, and hospital records require OCR, table-aware parsing, and metadata tagging so answers remain accurate.
- **Deterministic Tool Calling & Error Handling:** Connecting an AI agent to live production systems (like an [Industrial ERP](/services#industrial-erp), Stripe, or Salesforce) requires idempotency keys, retry queues, rate-limit protection, and human-approval checkpoints for high-stakes actions.
- **Evaluation & Guardrails:** Production systems include automated test suites that verify citation grounding and block prompt injections before a single customer interacts with your bot.

## AI Chatbot vs. RAG Assistant vs. AI Agent: Which One Do You Need?

Many businesses overpay for complex multi-agent frameworks when a targeted RAG pipeline or deterministic workflow automation solves 90% of the bottleneck faster.

1. **Rule-Based or Prompt-Only Chatbot (Legacy):** Follows static decision trees or sends raw prompts to an LLM without internal company data. *Best avoided for B2B operations due to high hallucination risk.*
2. **Custom RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Copilot:** Connects an LLM to your verified private documents, SOPs, product catalogs, and databases. Every answer is grounded in retrieved excerpts with source citations—eliminating guesswork.
3. **Autonomous AI Workflow Agent:** Combines reasoning with secure API tools. An AI agent does not just answer a customer or employee—it **executes tasks**, such as reading an inbound purchase order PDF, validating stock in PostgreSQL, drafting a quote, updating your CRM, and notifying a manager on Slack or WhatsApp for one-click approval.

> **Engineering Rule of Thumb:** If your bottleneck is *finding accurate answers inside thousands of files*, build a **Custom RAG Assistant**. If your bottleneck is *copying data, qualifying leads, generating documents, and updating systems manually*, build an **AI Workflow Agent**.
